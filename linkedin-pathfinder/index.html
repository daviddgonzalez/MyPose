Intro Pathfinder

Find the shortest, warmest chain of introductions from you to the people you want to reach.

1. Import your 1st-degree connections

LinkedIn → Settings → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data → Connections. Upload the Connections.csv you get.

2. Add a target

Someone you want to reach, with the degree LinkedIn shows for them.

3. Record who knows whom

Use this for 3rd / 3rd+ targets: e.g. a 2nd-degree coworker of theirs, and which of your connections knows that coworker.
